      About us

      Home cooking, at function scale

      Amma's Virundhu cooks home-style South Indian vegetarian food, the kind families grow up with. You tell us what you like, and we plan the menu around your taste, your family tradition and your guests. Every dish is cooked fresh in our own kitchen and brought hot to your function, so it tastes the way it does at home.

      From a small pooja to a family function of up to 60 guests, we can cover the whole day: morning breakfast, lunch on the banana leaf, evening mini tiffin and dinner. We cook in our kitchen, bring the food hot, serve your guests and clean up, so your family can be with your guests.
